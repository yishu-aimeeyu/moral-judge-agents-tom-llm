## E5d — span-matched surface control (final attempt at P4's gate)

Pre-registered in [`E5_PRESPEC.md`](../experiments/E5_PRESPEC.md) (R4/R5). The surface predictor is rebuilt from the **setup span only** — `text[:belief_start]` from `clause_offsets.csv`, the portion preceding either manipulation, following `44_span_matched_gaps.py`'s definition of the non-factor span. If full-document TF-IDF failed because it absorbed the lexically-realised factors, restricting it to text that realises neither factor is the fix.

- **P1 at relative depth ≥ 0.75**: **undecidable** — every layer was excluded by P4 (surface-dominated), so the comparison set is empty in this variant. This is not evidence in either direction.
- **P2 at the peak-intent layer**: **undecidable** — every layer was excluded by P4 (surface-dominated), so the comparison set is empty in this variant. This is not evidence in either direction.

**It did not fix it, and this is the final answer.** All 36/36 gated (model, layer) cells — every late-depth layer and every peak-intent layer — are still P4-excluded. At late layers the span-matched surface predictor's median unique ρ² is **0.08389**, against 0.00107 for outcome and 0.00008 for intent — roughly 78× the former and 1118× the latter.

The reason is the one recorded in the prespec **before** this ran, and it is the same reason full-document TF-IDF failed. The setup span is nearly identical across the four cells of a scenario and differs sharply between scenarios, so its word-overlap distance is close to the scenario-identity matrix — which E1 already showed dominates the RDM. Removing the factor clauses does not remove scenario identity; it concentrates it.

### E1 as a methods note

**Surface-control gating is undefined when the experimental factors are lexically realised.** In a within-scenario 2×2 whose conditions are written as clause substitutions, any word-level surface predictor sits *between* the factors and the representation: it is not a nuisance variable that can be held constant, because holding it constant would hold the manipulation constant too. P4 was a reasonable gate to pre-register and it is unsatisfiable in this design, at both the document and the span level. E1's registered P1/P2 are therefore **undecidable, not refuted** — a design finding rather than a result about models, and the honest thing to report.

The 3–18× outcome-over-intent dominance remains labelled **post-hoc (M1)** everywhere it appears, including in every downstream document. It is not promoted by this section: M1 drops the gate that E1 could not satisfy, which is exactly why it cannot inherit P1's pre-registered status.
