## Reading this against the pre-registered table

The result is split across models, so no pooled claim is made. The mechanism is not established in the models whose test arm fails. What the split is made of, model by model:

| model | pattern | what it means |
|---|---|---|
| `OLMo-2-1124-7B-Instruct` | `A` | **(A)-shaped.** The recovery survives removing the scenario-aligned component, so the overlap is incidental in this model. |
| `Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct` | `void` | **Void, not negative.** The positive control does not clear the floor at the matched displacement, so this model's comparison carries no information about (A) vs (B). E7_PRESPEC wrote this row in advance so a null here could not be read as support for (A). |
| `Llama-3.1-Tulu-3-8B` | `falsifier` | **(B)-shaped — this is the combination E7_PRESPEC named in advance as falsifying (A).** The positive control and the complement both clear the floor at this displacement and the test arm does not: the scenario-aligned component carries the effect and the orthogonal component alone does not. |

### The floor is a threshold, and one model sits just under it

`Llama-3.1-Tulu-3-8B`'s test arm reaches 0.0941, which is 95% of the 0.0988 floor, against a complement at 0.1008 that clears it. The two components are within 0.0067 of each other and the floor falls between them.

So the honest reading of that model is not that the orthogonal component does nothing. It is that both components move the readout by a similar amount, neither one clearly on its own, and a pre-registered threshold had to be placed somewhere. Calling this (B) would lean on the third decimal place of a permutation quantile. The pre-registered table is applied as written, and the narrowness is reported here rather than left for a reader to notice.

### What this closes, and what it does not authorize

The mechanism thread closes here as promised, and it closes with a bound rather than a mechanism: on this evidence the scenario-aligned part of the outcome direction cannot be dismissed as incidental in all three models, so W8A's recovery should not be described as outcome-specific without qualification.
