# E5 — controls hardening W8: verdict

Generated by `code/experiments/65_e5_controls.py` from the CSVs written by `63_w8_amnesic_outcome.py --run --nuisance --select-site`. Thresholds were fixed in [`E5_PRESPEC.md`](E5_PRESPEC.md) before that run.

## Headline after E5

**2 of 3 models show RECOVERY**, not 3 of 3. `OLMo-2-1124-7B-Instruct` RECOVERY (+0.3182); `Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct` RECOVERY (+0.2312); `Llama-3.1-Tulu-3-8B` COLLAPSE (+0.3480).

**E5a's primary control fails in `Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct`.** A morally irrelevant direction (scenario identity), erased at a perturbation norm matched to the outcome arm, moves the contrast past the J4 floor. Every breaching dose also fails the pre-registered coherence bound, and at coherent doses the nuisance arms stay below the floor — but that restriction was not pre-registered, so the pre-registered verdict is a failure and the claim narrows: the outcome direction is **the most effective** direction found, not the **only** one.

**E5c clears the one number that looked fragile.** OLMo's effect survives spread floors of 0.55 and 0.60, so it is not produced by cell compression, and OLMo is not downgraded.

**What survives intact.** Erasing outcome moves the contrast toward positive by far more than any nuisance direction at equal perturbation norm (10.3× in OLMo, 4.3× in Tulu), the dose-response is monotone in all three models, and the intent coefficient is preserved throughout. **What does not survive** is the strong form of the claim — that the effect is specific to the outcome direction and reproduces as recovery in every model.

## E5a — decodable-nuisance erasure

Erasing morally irrelevant directions at the same site, at two dose conventions: **exact** (erase the factor) and **norm-matched** (scale until mean displacement equals the outcome arm's at γ=1.0). Thresholds fixed in [`E5_PRESPEC.md`](E5_PRESPEC.md).

> **The structural caveat, restated because it bounds what E5a can prove.** The 2×2 is manipulated *within* scenario, so every morally irrelevant property is a property *of* the scenario and constant across its four cells. No nuisance factor here can reach outcome's leave-group-out decodability (0.99); the best available are length (0.65) and gender (0.56). E5a therefore matches on **perturbation norm**, which is the gap O1 identified, and reports decodability rather than matching it. A reader should not read E5a as a decodability-matched control, because no such control exists in this design.

Only the γ=1.0 row of each arm is tabulated; the full dose grid is in `w8_nuisance_<tag>.csv`. Decodability is leave-scenario-group-out CV accuracy at the erasure layer; `scenario` is multi-class and its held-out groups are unseen classes, so its V1a is measured **in-sample** and it has no leave-group-out number.

> **A correction to the sweep's own gate column, disclosed rather than silently applied.** `w8_nuisance_<tag>.csv` records `v1a_guarded = True` for the norm-matched arms while also recording the erased factor's in-sample accuracy as 1.000. Both cannot be true, and the accuracy is the trustworthy number. The cause is the dose convention: norm-matching scales the projection by `s > 1` (2.47× for gender, 1.53× for length, 1.28× for scenario in OLMo), which reflects the residual *past* the class mean rather than onto it, so the factor becomes linearly decodable again with inverted sign instead of being erased. The **state of erased factor** column above is recomputed here from the accuracy and the factor's majority rate. The consequence for interpretation is stated once and applies throughout: **the norm-matched arms are matched-magnitude perturbations, not erasures.** That is what the prespec said they would answer — *does a perturbation this large move judgment, wherever it points* — so P1 and P2 are unaffected, but no norm-matched row should be described as an erasure of its factor. The `exact` gender and length arms *are* genuine erasures: they land on 0.530 and 0.547, each within 0.05 of the factor's majority rate.

> The `scenario` arm is a separate case and never was an erasure at any dose. Scenario identity has ~75 classes, and a rank-1 projection cannot collapse a 75-class structure, so in-sample accuracy stays at 1.000 even at `s = 1`. The prespec chose it deliberately as *the most decodable structure in the representation* and therefore the strongest available form of the objection; what it delivers is a large perturbation along the RDM's dominant axis, which is exactly the stress test P1 needed, but it is a **perturbation of** scenario structure, not a removal of it.

### The P1 breach in `Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct`, stated plainly

- **scenario**, norm-matched dose, γ=1.0: Δcontrast **+0.1345** ≥ 0.0988. Cell spread retained 0.5510; perplexity ×1.600; V3 coherence **FAIL**.

**Two facts about this breach, and neither cancels it.** Every breaching dose fails W8's pre-registered V3 coherence bound (perplexity ≤ 1.5×), so the model is measurably degraded where the floor is crossed, and W8's own P1 counts only gate-passing doses. Restricted to V3-coherent doses, the largest nuisance movement is **+0.0929** (scenario, norm-matched, γ=0.75), which is below the floor — so on a like-for-like comparison with the outcome arm the nuisance arms are inert.

That is a real distinction and it is still **not** a pass. The prespec did not restrict P1 to gate-passing doses, so the literal verdict stands, and the gate-restricted number above is secondary by construction. The honest reading is the narrower one: **the contrast is not moved only by the outcome direction.** A large perturbation along scenario identity moves it too, and at the strongest coherent dose it reaches 94% of the floor, which is close enough that W8's specificity claim must be stated as *relative*, not absolute.

**P3 (decodability disclosure).** Satisfied by the table above: every erased factor's leave-group-out accuracy sits beside its Δcontrast, so the distance from a decodability-matched control is visible rather than argued. No verdict rides on P3.

## E5b — third model (`Llama-3.1-Tulu-3-8B`)

Site chosen **in-run and before any behavioural readout** by W3's own rule — the layer maximising leave-scenario-group-out intent probe accuracy on the chat-templated last-token residual: **L17** (intent CV accuracy 0.9161, swept over 33 layers; full sweep in `w8_amnesic_layer_selection_Llama-3.1-Tulu-3-8B.csv`). There is no W3 baseline contrast for this model, so the W8 drift cross-check is **n/a**, not a passing check.

Rank calibration selected rank 1 (`leace(rank=1)`): V1a in-sample outcome 0.5067, V1b held-out 0.5038, intent retention 1.0.

|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| 0.25 | +0.0899 | 0.8286 | 1.002 | fail | NO CHANGE |
| 0.5 | +0.1768 | 0.6658 | 1.011 | fail | RECOVERY |
| 0.75 | +0.2628 | 0.5127 | 1.031 | fail | RECOVERY |
| 1.0 | +0.3480 | 0.3753 | 1.067 | pass | COLLAPSE |

- **P1 (replication)**: Δcontrast +0.3480 at the gate-passing dose ≥ 0.0988 → **supported**.
- **P2 (label)**: **COLLAPSE** — cell spread retained 0.3753 against the 50% rule.

**This is the falsification branch the prespec named, and it changes the headline.** The contrast moves well past the floor, but the four cell means compress to 38% of their original spread, below the 50% rule, so the movement is **COLLAPSE**: it comes from losing outcome discrimination rather than from intent taking over. Per E5_PRESPEC.md the headline is therefore **2 of 3 models, not 3 of 3**, and that belongs in the abstract's headline sentence rather than in a limitations paragraph.

**Post-hoc, and disclosed for symmetry with E5c — it does not change the label.** The COLLAPSE label rests on the γ=1.0 dose, the only one that satisfies V1a. Applying E5c's lens to this model, at γ=0.75 the contrast has moved +0.2628 while cell spread is still 0.5127, i.e. above the 50% rule — the RECOVERY signature. That dose does **not** satisfy V1a (outcome is not fully erased), so per the prespec it cannot count, and this model stays **COLLAPSE** and stays outside the headline's 2. It is recorded because E5c's sensitivity lens was pre-registered for OLMo, where it helped; applying it to this model only when it hurts, or suppressing it when it helps, would both be selective. What it licenses is a bounded statement: the third model's failure is a failure *at full erasure*, and whether it differs in kind from the other two or only in how much erasure it tolerates is not resolvable here.

## E5c — spread-matched sensitivity for `OLMo-2-1124-7B-Instruct`

W8's gate-passing dose for this model retained only 0.5124 of the pre-erasure cell spread, 0.0124 above the COLLAPSE boundary. Δcontrast is recomputed under stricter spread floors. **Sensitivity analysis only**: sub-maximal doses do not satisfy V1a and cannot count toward W8's P1.

| spread floor | eligible doses | largest Δcontrast | ≥ J4 floor |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| > 0.55 | 3 | +0.2355 (γ=0.75, spread 0.6581) | **yes** |
| > 0.60 | 3 | +0.2355 (γ=0.75, spread 0.6581) | **yes** |

- **P1 (survives > 0.55)**: **supported**.
- **P2 (survives > 0.60)**: **supported**.

The effect is **not** an artefact of near-maximal cell compression. At γ=0.75 the cells still retain 66% of their spread — comfortably clear of the COLLAPSE rule — and the contrast has already moved +0.2355, 2.4× the J4 floor. The pre-registered consequence (downgrade to supporting evidence) is therefore **not** triggered, and `OLMo-2-1124-7B-Instruct` stays as primary evidence.
