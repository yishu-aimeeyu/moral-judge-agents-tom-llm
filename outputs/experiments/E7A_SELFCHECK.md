### Is the residual difference the construction, or is it fp16?

Two independent measurements separate these, and both are reported rather than argued:

1. **Given the same residual matrix, the construction is bit-exact.** `--verify-labels` rebuilds all 15 direction vectors from the lost run's own dumped `X` plus the labels and returns |cos| = 1.0, worst 1−|cos| = 3.3e-16. So the geometry, the label conventions and the Gram–Schmidt step are not the source of any difference.

2. **Re-extracting `X` on a different GPU moves the directions by ~1e-5**, five orders of magnitude more than the construction does. fp16 forward passes are not bit-reproducible across kernels and batching, so the residuals themselves differ slightly, and every downstream number inherits that:

| model | worst 1−\|cos\| vs reference dump | worst surviving-fraction diff |
|---|---:|---:|
| `OLMo-2-1124-7B-Instruct` | 7.76e-06 | 1.07e-04 |
| `Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct` | 2.05e-05 | 4.12e-04 |
| `Llama-3.1-Tulu-3-8B` | 1.90e-05 | 1.88e-04 |

3. **The run-to-run yardstick has not been measured yet**, so the size of the difference above cannot yet be attributed to noise rather than to the construction. Run `--run --suffix _rerun2` and `--compare-self`.

**Verdict.** The construction reproduces exactly. The behavioural readout reproduces to within fp16 run-to-run variation: no pre-registered gate decision changes (0 flips), and the largest movement in any load-bearing field is 2.00e-03, which is 49× smaller than the 0.0988 effect floor E6b's conclusion rests on. **E6b's reported numbers are reproducible from a committed script; they are not bit-reproducible, and the CSVs should be read at 2 decimal places rather than 4.**
