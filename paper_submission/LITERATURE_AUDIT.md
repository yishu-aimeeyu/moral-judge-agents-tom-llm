## Provenance clarification

The project-local Bruneau stimulus filename contains “2011.” The article was available online in December 2011 and appeared in the 2012 journal issue; the bibliography's 2012 year is therefore retained. The supplement states this explicitly.

OmniToM, OpenToM, and unattributed claims found in older project planning notes are not cited or used in the manuscript because they were not needed for the argument and were not part of the verified bibliography.
